01 / Security · Explainable ML

Explainable phishing detection under distribution shift

A hybrid ensemble that catches phishing websites, explains every decision and knows when it isn’t sure. It is tested on live URLs it never saw during training.

Problem

Phishing detectors score well on benchmarks but quietly fail when attacks change, and they rarely say why.

Approach

Stacking ensembles with calibrated probabilities, conformal prediction and SHAP TreeExplainer.

Evaluation

Trained on UCI Phishing Websites, then drift-tested on real JPCERT/CC URLs, with calibration and selective prediction.

Outcome

Submitted to ECCE 2027. CSE 477 Data Mining project.

02 / Medical imaging · Research

CoroHybridNet: finding narrowed coronary arteries

A hybrid deep-plus-traditional pipeline that fuses CNN feature maps with multiscale Frangi vesselness filtering to detect coronary stenosis in X-ray angiography.

Problem

Reading angiogram sequences for stenosis is slow and subjective, and pure CNNs can miss thin, low-contrast vessels.

Approach

Adaptive fusion of learned CNN features with hand-crafted multiscale Frangi vesselness maps.

Evaluation

CADICA dataset with patient-separated splits so there is no leakage, benchmarked against CoroGraphST.

Status

In progress, aiming for a top-venue paper.

03 / Computer vision · Capstone project

Calliper (TailorCam): an AI tailor in your webcam

Stand in front of a webcam and get tailor-ready body measurements in centimetres. No tape, no appointment.

Problem

Getting measured is a barrier for online and custom tailoring, and 2D keypoints alone give no real-world scale.

Approach

Live MediaPipe Pose, metric calibration, then SMPL-X 3D body fitting to extract circumferences.

Engineering

About 2,500 lines of Python. I fixed the rotation-tracker maths and the aspect-ratio scaling bugs.

Validation

Anthropometric references explored: ANSUR II and CAESAR. PDF export and email-to-tailor.
